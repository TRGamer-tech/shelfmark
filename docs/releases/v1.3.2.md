Debug release. Install it over the version you have, your library stays where it is.

## New

- **The confirmation mail ends on a page that says so.** Where a new account has to confirm its e-mail address, the link in the mail now leads to a page of the Shelfmark website: "Your e-mail address is confirmed", and what to do next. The app names that page itself when it creates an account, so the mail no longer depends on a setting of the server that could point somewhere else.
- **Privacy notice and imprint.** Both are on the website, and one tap away in the app: under the form that creates an account, and in the settings.
