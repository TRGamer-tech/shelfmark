Debug release. Install it over the version you have, your library stays where it is.

**Before you install it:** the server needs the new `supabase/schema.sql` (run it once more in the SQL editor of the Supabase project). Until then signing in does not work. Everything else does.

## One library, everywhere

- **An account.** Sign in with e-mail and password. Your library is then kept in step with your account: a change made on another phone or on the website arrives here by itself, and a change made here goes there a few seconds later.
- **The app asks for it at the first start.** A new installation opens with "Sign in" and "Create account". Signing in to an account that has books brings them along with name and looks, and leads straight to the shelf. Whoever has the app already finds the sign-in in the Friends tab and in the settings.
- **The website.** The same library in a browser, with the same sign-in. It does what the app does, reading timer and import from Goodreads or StoryGraph included. What it leaves to the phone is what needs a phone: the camera (scanning a barcode, reading a quote off a page), zip backups with the cover photos in them, and notifications.
- **The shelf looks the same on every device.** Lists, ornaments, the theme and your own themes, the view (spines, covers or list), the sort order and the movement switch all belong to the account.
- **Cover photos travel too.** A cover you photographed is uploaded, so the website, your other devices and your friends see it.
- When two devices changed things while the other was not looking, both sets of changes are kept, book by book. An account and a device that each have their own books ask once which library counts.
- It still goes without an account ("Continue without an account"). Then the app works as before, on this device only.

## Simpler

- **Friends go through the account.** Signing in is all it takes to get a friend code. The separate "connect" step is gone.
- The two settings that only made the app harder to use are gone: the Google Books key and the server of one's own. The app talks to the server it was built for.

## Fixed

- Importing a list that names the same book twice: what the first row added to a book you already had is no longer lost when the second row comes, and the summary counts books, not rows.

## Good to know

- The profiles of 1.2.0 were tied to one phone and cannot be turned into accounts. After signing in, connect with your friends once more with the new friend codes.
- Deleting the account (in the settings) removes everything from the server, cover photos included. The books on the phone stay.
