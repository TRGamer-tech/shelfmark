Debug release. Install it over 1.0.0, your library stays where it is.

## New

- **Seven book catalogues instead of two**: Open Library, Google Books, Apple Books, lobid, Deutsche Nationalbibliothek, K10plus and swisscovery are asked at once and their answers merged. German-language and older editions are found far more often.
- **Covers**: a book that arrives without a cover gets a search in the background. "Search cover online" on the edit page shows every cover the catalogues have. Settings has a tool that goes through all books still missing one.
- **Calendar** with the covers of each day's reading and a heatmap of the year.
- **Recaps** for every month and year, and **milestones** in eight categories.
- **Purchase log** per book: price, shop, date.
- **Shelf ornaments**: twelve things to put between the books of any shelf.
- **Themes of your own** from four colours, next to the 18 presets.
- **Import** from Goodreads, StoryGraph and any CSV list. Books you already have are completed, not duplicated.
- The reading timer moved to a small button on the Reading tab of a book.
