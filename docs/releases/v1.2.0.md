Debug release. Install it over 1.0.0 or 1.1.0, your library stays where it is.

## Friends without sending files

- **A server for friends.** Connect once in the Friends tab, hand out your friend code, and that is it: friends see the shelf you share, and borrow requests and their answers arrive by themselves. A request that comes in while the app is closed is announced with a notification.
- Files still work, for friends who are not connected.
- The server is a Supabase project of your own. What it stores and who can read it is described in `supabase/schema.sql`. The app checks in with it once a day so that a free project is not paused.

## A shelf that feels like one

- **Lists of your own are shelves.** With "All" selected, each of your lists stands below the built-in ones as a shelf of its own, the empty ones too.
- Lamplight falls on the shelf and dust drifts in it. Books settle into place when the shelf opens, rise under your finger, and the last book of a shelf that is not full leans on its neighbour.
- Ornaments are alive: the candle flickers, the tea steams, leaves stir, the clock tells the time. Two new ones: a sleeping cat and a stack of books.
- The shelf greets you and offers the book you are reading.
- All movement can be switched off under Settings > A lively shelf, and stays off where the phone has animations disabled.

## Since 1.0.0

- **Seven book catalogues instead of two**: Open Library, Google Books, Apple Books, lobid, Deutsche Nationalbibliothek, K10plus and swisscovery are asked at once and their answers merged.
- **Covers**: a book that arrives without a cover gets a search in the background, and the edit page shows every cover the catalogues have.
- **Calendar**, **recaps** for every month and year, and **milestones**.
- **Purchase log**, **shelf ornaments** and **themes of your own**.
- **Import** from Goodreads, StoryGraph and any CSV list.
- The reading timer moved to a small button on the Reading tab of a book.
