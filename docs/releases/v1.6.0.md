Debug release. Install it over the version you have, your library stays where it is. The server needs nothing new.

## New

- **The book cart.** Adding seven Harry Potter volumes no longer means searching seven times. Every search result can go onto the book cart with one tap, and so can a book you look up by ISBN or type in by hand. The scanner has a switch that puts every book it finds straight onto the cart and stays ready for the next one, so a whole pile of books is scanned in one go. On the cart you set the list, the ownership and your own lists for all books at once, change them for single books, and shelve them all with one tap. A book that is already on your shelf is marked and left out. The cart stays on the device until you shelve or empty it.
- **Books that are away look away.** A book you lent out stands see-through with a dashed outline until it is back. So does a borrowed book you gave back before you finished it; marked as read, it is solid like every other book. On the shelf, in the covers, in the list and on the book page, in the app and on the website alike.

## Better

- **Swiping back** from the edge of the screen no longer shrinks the page into a floating card. The page follows your finger to the side, and the one below appears.
