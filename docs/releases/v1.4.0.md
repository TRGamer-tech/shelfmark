## Borrowing, for real

A borrow request used to be a note passed between two phones, and nothing followed from it. A loan is now one record that both of you see, and every step changes something.

- **Ask.** On a friend's shelf, as before. The friend finds your question on the book itself and under **Loans**, which replaces the list of requests.
- **Yes means reserved.** Nobody else can ask for the book now. With the yes comes a day the book is wanted back by, or "no fixed date". A no can carry a reason, and if somebody else got the book you are told so and can ask again later.
- **Handing over takes both of you.** The lender says "I handed it over", the borrower says "I have it". Only then is the book lent.
- **The borrowed book stands on your own shelf**, as borrowed, ready to be read, rated and quoted from.
- **Giving back takes both of you too.** The borrower says "I gave it back", the lender says "I have it back". The lender's book is free again, and the borrower keeps their copy as "read, not mine", with everything they noted in it.
- **A reminder when the day has come**, for both. The lender can change the day at any time.
- Either side can step back as long as the book has not changed hands, and you can always ask again.
- The app now looks for news every minute while it is open, not only when it is opened.

Borrowing needs both sides signed in. Showing a shelf as a file to somebody without an account still works, and so does lending by hand to somebody who does not use Shelfmark.

## Safer, after an independent review

Somebody who had not written a line of Shelfmark read all of it and looked for what is plain bad. What they found and what is fixed:

- **A sync could run over what you had just done.** Stars tapped or a session logged while the app was fetching the library from the server could be lost. The library is now only replaced if it is still the one the sync looked at.
- **An open page could write old text back.** A review written on the website could be overwritten by the empty field of a book page that was still open on the phone. Fields now follow what is stored until you type, and the edit form saves only what you changed in it.
- **A library the app cannot read is never overwritten** (a newer version wrote it, or it is damaged). The app says so and waits for an update instead.
- **"Which library counts?" no longer throws one away for good.** The one that loses is kept as a backup on the phone, and the settings can bring it back.
- **Android's own backup is switched off for Shelfmark.** It took the library and the sign-in to Google's cloud while the app said that everything stays on the device. The backup in the settings and the account are the ways to move a library.
- **Signing out signs out this phone only**, not every device. If a sign-in ends by itself, the shelf says so.
- **Cover photos you took were never uploaded by the app**, although the tests said they were. They are now, and a photo is removed from the server when no book shows it any more. Deleting the account removes all of them.
- **No more crash from a number that is none** (a series position of 1e39), and nothing that goes wrong in a sync closes the app.

Smaller things: undo after moving a book to another list, a question before something is deleted or typed text is thrown away, stars and chillies a screen reader can use, a large import that no longer freezes the app, and texts in the settings that say what the app really does.
