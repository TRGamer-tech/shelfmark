Debug release. Install it over the version you have, your library stays where it is.

**Before you install it:** the server needs the new `supabase/schema.sql` once more (SQL editor of the Supabase project). It keeps everything that is there. Until then the app works as before, only the list of earlier versions says that the server does not keep any yet, and nobody is told about a friend who joins.

## New

- **You are told when somebody joins.** Whoever types in your friend code is your friend at once, and until now you were not told. Now the phone says "Anna is now your friend", and from there it is one tap to remove somebody you do not know.
- **Notifications that arrive.** The app asked for the permission only once, right after signing in. Whoever dismissed that, or was signed in before, got nothing and did not know. The Friends tab now says when notifications are off and switches them on with one tap. In the background the app now looks for news about every quarter of an hour instead of twice a day (Android decides when exactly, there is no push service).
- **Earlier versions of your library.** The server keeps up to ten: a few from the last hours and one for each of the last days. The settings list them and bring one back on every device. What you have at that moment is kept in turn, so bringing back can itself be taken back.
- **"Which library counts?" can now mean both.** When the first sign-in on a phone finds books on the phone and on the account, a third choice puts the two together: the same book becomes one with everything both copies know, and every reading session, quote, note and list of either side is kept. Both libraries are put aside as backups first.

On the website: the same notice when somebody joins, the same list of earlier versions, and, if you allow it, notifications of the browser for news from friends while Shelfmark is open in a tab that is not in front.
