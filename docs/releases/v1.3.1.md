Debug release. Install it over the version you have, your library stays where it is.

## New

- **A link to the website.** The settings now lead to Shelfmark in the browser, where the same library is waiting behind the same sign-in.
- **Back from the confirmation mail, the app signs you in by itself.** Where a new account has to confirm its e-mail address first, nobody has to guess what comes after the link in the mail any more: come back to the app and you are in. The website does the same and says that the address is confirmed.
