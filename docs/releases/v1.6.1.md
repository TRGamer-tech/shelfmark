Debug release. Install it over the version you have, your library stays where it is. The server needs nothing new.

## Fixed

- **Wishes stand see-through too.** A book on your wishlist, a copy from the library and any other book that is not yours was drawn solid, as if it stood on your shelf. Now it is see-through with a dashed outline like a book you lent out. The same goes for a book you moved to the wishlist that still said "I own it".
- **Every combination checked.** Which books are see-through is now one table of all 92 combinations of list, ownership, lending and borrowing, and the app and the website are both tested against it:
  - see-through: lent out (in every list, until it is back), not yours and not read, and on the wishlist while it says it is yours;
  - solid: yours (except on the wishlist), borrowed while you have it, and every book you have read that is not lent out.
- **A wish cannot be borrowed.** A book on the wishlist that still said "I own it" was offered to friends to borrow. It no longer is.
